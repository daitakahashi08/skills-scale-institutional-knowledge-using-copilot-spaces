# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure features meet quality standards before release. They collaborate with developers and product teams to define testability requirements and manage test execution.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test strategy and plans for validating acceptance criteria
- Create and maintain test cases and automated test suites
- Coordinate test execution across unit, integration, end-to-end, and manual QA
- Manage QA environments and test data
- Report quality metrics, defect trends, and test status
- Escalate quality risks or blockers and contribute to the Definition of Done

### Goals
- Catch quality issues early and reduce production defects
- Enable fast, confident releases through effective test coverage
- Maintain transparency on quality status and risks

### Typical Communication
- Provide testability input during backlog refinement and sprint planning
- Share QA progress, defects, and blockers in daily standups
- Give feedback on test coverage in pull request reviews
- Report quality metrics and release readiness to project and product leads

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural direction, mentor development teams, and ensure solutions meet technical standards for scalability, security, and maintainability. They bridge technical and business perspectives in planning and execution.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and document significant architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and feasibility concerns
- Mentor developers on implementation patterns and best practices
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and technical policies
- Advise product and project leads on trade-offs between speed, quality, and scope

### Goals
- Deliver architecturally sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and avoid preventable rework
- Enable team learning and consistent engineering practices

### Typical Communication
- Lead design reviews and technical discussions with developers
- Provide implementation guidance through code reviews and mentoring
- Update the project risk register with technical risks and mitigations
- Give feasibility and dependency input during planning sessions

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business interests, approve project charters, and make high-level decisions. They provide business context and success criteria, authorize resources, and receive regular status updates.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charters, scope, and resource allocation
- Define business priorities and provide input on success measures
- Make escalated decisions on business-impacting scope, schedule, and resource trade-offs
- Review project status and release readiness at agreed approval gates
- Communicate project benefits and decisions to the wider organization

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Remove organizational blockers and enable timely decisions
- Maintain executive visibility and alignment on project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Receive weekly or milestone-based status reports from the Project Manager
- Participate in project kickoff and approval gates for initiation, planning, and release
- Respond to escalations and decision requests from project and product leads
- Review post-release outcomes against business success measures

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security standards, regulatory requirements, and incident response protocols. They review designs for vulnerabilities and guide secure development practices.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements and acceptance criteria
- Review designs and advise on security risks and mitigations
- Coordinate security reviews, vulnerability scanning, and penetration testing as needed
- Track regulatory requirements and support audit readiness
- Maintain incident response guidance and advise during security incidents
- Escalate unresolved security and compliance risks to project leadership

### Goals
- Protect user data and system integrity
- Maintain compliance with applicable regulations and standards
- Integrate security into planning, development, and release practices

### Typical Communication
- Provide security input during design reviews and backlog planning
- Coordinate with developers on security findings and remediation
- Record security risks and mitigations in the project risk register
- Notify Security on-call and project leadership during security incidents
- Share audit and compliance status with project sponsors as needed

---

## Release Manager / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Release Managers and DevOps Engineers manage CI/CD pipelines, coordinate release schedules, and ensure production readiness. They enable reliable, repeatable deployments and support operational excellence.

### Responsibilities
- Design, maintain, and monitor CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Coordinate release schedules, deployment windows, and release communications
- Verify pre-release requirements, including CI, security scans, and rollback plans
- Manage infrastructure, monitoring, and deployment configuration
- Execute or support deployments and verify production readiness
- Monitor post-release health and coordinate rollback or incident response when needed

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and reliable deployments
- Minimize deployment risk and production downtime
- Maintain observability and effective incident response capability

### Typical Communication
- Coordinate release plans and readiness with project and technical leads
- Confirm acceptance criteria, CI, and security scan status with QA and Security
- Communicate deployment windows and release updates to stakeholders and support
- Share post-deployment health status and coordinate incident response with on-call teams
