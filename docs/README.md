# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the central index for OctoAcme's project management process
guidance. These documents explain how cross-functional projects move from an
initial idea through planning, delivery, release, and continuous improvement.
Use them as practical guidance and checklists, adapting the team rhythm to the
needs of each project.

## How OctoAcme runs projects

OctoAcme starts by confirming the customer and business need, desired outcomes,
and success measures. Once stakeholders align and the work is approved, the
team plans a prioritized backlog of small, testable increments, with clear
acceptance criteria, ownership, milestones, and risks. During delivery, the
team tracks work, tests and reviews changes, communicates progress and
blockers, and uses evidence to guide decisions. Releases include readiness
checks and post-deployment verification; retrospectives turn experience into
owned improvement actions.

## Key principles

- **Customer-first:** Prioritize customer value and usability.
- **Iterative delivery:** Deliver small, testable increments and learn as the
  work progresses.
- **Clear ownership:** Name project and product leads, owners for work, and
  owners for risks and follow-up actions.
- **Data-informed decisions:** Define success measures and use evidence to
  evaluate impact and adjust priorities.
- **Psychological safety:** Encourage candid feedback, learning, and
  blameless reflection.

## Quick reference: key roles

| Role | Primary focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Project Manager (PM)** | Coordinates delivery, plans and timelines, risks, dependencies, and project and stakeholder communications. |
| **Product Manager (PdM)** | Defines customer and business outcomes, prioritizes the roadmap and backlog, and measures success. |
| **Developers** | Design, implement, test, and deliver changes; collaborate on estimates, reviews, and technical risks. |
| **QA / Testing** | Validates quality and acceptance criteria through the agreed test approach and feature acceptance checks. |

See [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) for more detail.

## Communication cadence

- **PM and PdM:** Weekly alignment.
- **Delivery team:** Standups and a weekly delivery sync. The overview suggests
  twice-weekly standups (or as agreed); the execution guide recommends daily
  15-minute standups. Set the rhythm that fits the team.
- **Stakeholders:** Monthly updates as a baseline, or weekly / milestone-based
  updates to meet stakeholder needs.
- **Escalations:** Raise blockers through the team, PM, Product Lead, and
  sponsor as appropriate; communicate urgent issues as they arise.

## Project lifecycle at a glance

1. **Initiate:** Confirm the problem, stakeholders, outcomes, success measures,
   and whether to proceed.
2. **Plan:** Agree scope, capacity, backlog, acceptance criteria, milestones,
   dependencies, and risks.
3. **Execute and track:** Build, test, review, demonstrate progress, and adapt
   based on delivery and outcome signals.
4. **Release:** Confirm readiness, deploy, verify, and communicate the release.
5. **Close and improve:** Reflect on the work and assign owners and due dates
   to improvement actions.

Use the linked lifecycle documents for detailed steps and checklists.
